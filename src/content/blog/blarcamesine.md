## ブラルカメシン — 結果を一度も投稿しなかった 13 試験、一つの法廷にしか立たなかった申請、そして最後の法廷になった取締役会

## 0. Before the story: the code did not exist

The candidate table queued this asset as **"AV-217 (Anavex, Rett syndrome)"**. On opening the primary sources, **even the premise's code name did not survive**.

- **"AV-217" appears zero times** in every Anavex filing cached from EDGAR — four 10-Ks, three 10-Qs, over 25 8-Ks. The molecule is **ANAVEX 2-73**, generic name **blarcamesine**. (The code "AV-101" belongs to a different company's L-4-chlorokynurenine entirely — a phantom next to a phantom.)
- The candidate note predicted "the company publicly attacks the FDA (2024–25)". **No such document exists in the cached record.** What exists instead: the FDA *advised the company to request a meeting* , and the company's new CEO promised to work "closely with both the FDA and the EMA" . The public war that did happen was **shareholders against the board** — PVG Asset Management demanding the replacement of the entire board of directors . The quarrel was predicted correctly in emotion, wrongly in direction.
- One premise survived: the compassionate-use grey zone — 91% open-label-extension enrollment and 93% continued compassionate use, cited approvingly in the company's own 10-Ks .

## 1. The shape of the record: thirteen trials, zero results

ClinicalTrials.gov carries **13 trials** of blarcamesine and its follow-on molecule ANAVEX3-71 run by or listing Anavex. As of 2026-09-11, the number of trials with posted results is **zero** . Not one — across twelve years, three indications, four randomized trials over 100 patients, and a trial that its sponsors described as "pivotal."

The register itself keeps the clock, and the clock was never wound down:

- **AD-004** (NCT03790709): Phase 2b/3, randomized, quadruple-blind, n=509, primary completion **2022-06-30**. lastVerifiedDate: 2022-07. The registry record of this trial has been **frozen for four years and two months** — the legal ledger says the trial completed and produced nothing .
- The Phase 2a (n=32), the AD open-label extension (n=300), all three Rett trials, the PDD program (n=132), the schizophrenia Phase 1 and the ongoing ACTIVE Phase 2 — all hasResults=false .

The company did publish: CTAD conference abstracts in December 2022, a peer-reviewed paper in January 2025, press releases, 10-K narrative. What it never used is the ledger that the statute names.

> 【要約】13 試験・**Results Posting 0 件**。AD-004（509 例・第 IIb/III 相）の CTG レコードは主完了 2022-06-30 の直後 **2022-07 で凍結**し、登録上「結果なしの完了」が 4 年 2 か月続く。会社は抄録・論文・決算・PR で公表し続けたが、**法律が指名した台帳だけは一度も使わなかった**。

## 2. One trial, three books that disagree: the Alzheimer's study

**AD-004** is best read as three documents describing one set of patients.

**Book 1 — the registration .** Primary outcomes: ADAS-Cog at 48 weeks, and ADCS-ADL (daily-function) at 48 weeks. CDR-SB: *secondary*.

**Book 2 — the announcement .** At CTAD December 2022, and in the FY2023 10-K, the headline statistic was not a mean difference but an **odds ratio against a threshold**: the odds of being "a responder" (not declining past −0.5 on ADAS-Cog) were **OR 1.84, p=0.015**; for ADCS-ADL (threshold +3.5) **OR 2.67, p=0.0255**. Mean-difference figures followed (ADAS −1.85, p=0.033; CDR-SB −0.42, p=0.040). The 10-K states that "**all statistical analyses were performed by an external consulting firm**." The same 10-K asserts the "co-primary endpoints ADAS-Cog and ADCS-ADL were met."

**Book 3 — the paper .** Published in the *Journal of Prevention of Alzheimer's Disease* in January 2025 — **2.5 years after primary completion** — the prespecified MMRM analysis of 462 randomized-and-analyzed patients reports:

| Outcome (48 wks, MMRM) | LSM difference (95% CI) | P | Registered role | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ADAS-Cog13 | **−2.027 (−3.522 to −0.533)** | **0.008** | primary | met |
| ADCS-ADL | +0.775 (−0.874 to 2.423) | **0.357** | **primary** | **not met** |
| CDR-SB | −0.483 (−0.853 to −0.114) | 0.010 | *secondary* | rescued the claim |

The paper's own sentence: the "co-primary outcome was met **under the multiplicity control rule**" — because ADAS-Cog13 and CDR-SB each cleared P<0.025, "**while ADCS-ADL did not reach significance**." In plain terms: **one of the two co-primary endpoints lost**, and a pre-specified *fallback to the secondary endpoint* saved the word "met." Meanwhile the announcement's OR 1.84 and the paper's −2.027 are **different statistical units computed on the same patients** — and in this case the unit system, not the drug, decided which sentences were true.

The paper also reports what the press release did not foreground: serious treatment-emergent adverse events in **16.7% of blarcamesine patients vs 10.1% of placebo** .

> 【要約】同一試験・同一患者に対して三冊の本が食い違う。「共主項目達成」の意味が三冊で違う——**単位系が正負を決う**。serious TEAE 16.7% vs 10.1% も論文側の数字。

## 3. The Rett trilogy: thirty-one, thirty-three, ninety-two

The Rett program is where the series' recurring device — **the promotion of the secondary** — reaches its purest form.

- **RS-001** (n=31): registered primary outcomes were **adverse events and pharmacokinetics** — a safety study. The efficacy headlines the company carried into every subsequent filing (RSBQ behavior responders 66.7% vs 10%, p=0.011; CGI-I p=0.014) are **all secondary endpoints** .
- **AVATAR RS-002** (n=33): registered as **Phase 3**, seven weeks, sites in Australia and the UK. Its primary efficacy outcome is a **"drug exposure–dependent response"** of the RSBQ — not the randomized between-group difference the phrase "Phase 3" conventionally promises. The company announced it "**met all primary and secondary efficacy endpoints**" (RSBQ p=0.037) .
- **EXCELLENCE RS-003** (children, n=92, 12 weeks): the registered primary (RSBQ change) came back at **−4.61, p=0.063 — not met**. The company's written record then does three things at once: it calls the secondary CGI-I a "co-primary" (the registry says secondary) [ vs ]; it foregrounds a **4-week timepoint that was not a registered primary timeframe** (−4.65, p=0.041); and it reports OLE enrollment of **91%** and continued compassionate use of **93%** under the label "real-world evidence" . There was no 8-K for this readout; the topline entered the record through the annual report.

The steelman is on the record too, in print: a 2025 *British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology* paper — "**The new big is small**" — models blarcamesine's Rett data with item-response theory and Bayes factors and argues small trials in rare disease *can* carry strength of evidence . Whatever else it is, Anavex's Rett program has become the specimen on which the rare-disease statistics debate is being conducted.

> 【要約】Rett 三部作は「二次項目の昇格」の純粋形。RS-001（n=31）の主項目は**安全と PK のみ**（有効性は全部二次）。AVATAR（n=33・**登録上第 III 相**）の主項目は「**曝露反応**」——第 III 相の通常の意味（割付ベースの対照差）ではない。EXCELLENCE（小児 92 例）は RSBQ **p=0.063 で未達**→会社は①登録上二次の CGI-I を「co-primary」と呼び②登録外の 4 週（p=0.041）を掲げ③OLE 91%・CUP 93% を「RWE」と呼ぶ（**8-K なし・決算経由**）。擁護論文（IRT＋ベイズ）も査読誌に実在。

## 4. One courtroom only: Europe convicted, America never sat

The FDA has **never received an application** for blarcamesine. openFDA returns NOT_FOUND on the label database, the applications database, and the adverse-event database for both spellings of the name (retrieved 2026-09-11) . There is no NDA, no CRL, no advisory committee — the regulatory layer in the United States is not a failed verdict; it is **an absence**. The only courtroom this asset ever stood in was the EMA's.

That courtroom's record, from the company's own filings :

- **November 2024**: MAA submitted for early Alzheimer's disease (add-on therapy). December 2024: validated.
- **November 14, 2025**: after the oral explanation, the CHMP issued a **negative trend vote**. The same press release carries two half-sentences about the FDA: "after initial dialogue, the U.S. FDA's CDER **advised the Company to request a meeting** to discuss the Company's Alzheimer's disease clinical trial results." The company announced it would request a **re-examination** of the opinion, "including providing relevant biomarker data."
- **March 25, 2026**: the MAA was **withdrawn** — the press release says CHMP "indicated that it would not be in a position to issue a positive opinion for the application at this time."  The EMA's own documents are unreachable: the EPAR page returns 404. The formal December 2025 opinion wording is **unverified** in this ledger.
- The pattern is a double of pridopidine: CHMP negative (July 2025) → re-examination requested → **withdrawn November 2025**. Four months apart, the same shape: *re-examination requested, never completed; withdrawal instead.*

And then, on **September 9, 2026**, the strangest document in the file: FDA **granted the company's request for a meeting** — about adding **pediatric patients** to RS-005, the planned adult Rett Phase 3 of **n≈170, randomized, double-blind, 12 weeks** . Read against the trilogy, this is the small-data experiment **reverting to exactly the classical design it had been arguing against** — while a 2026-09 Fragile-X IND waits in the wings. The retreat is quiet, but the shape of RS-005 says the quiet part.

> 【要約】米国は**申請が一度も存在しない**（openFDA 3 端点 NOT_FOUND）。裁かれたのは EU のみ——2024-11 MAA 提出→**2025-11-14 CHMP 否定傾向投票**→再審査表明→**2026-03-25 取下げ**。型は pridopidine と 4 か月違いの同型（再審査は完遂されない）。そして 2026-09-09、FDA が**小児組み入れの面談を承認**——計画中の RS-005 は **n≈170 の古典的無作為化二重盲検**。小データ擁護論の実験台が、静かに古典設計へ回帰している。

## 6. The strongest case for the defendant

An honest ledger records the steelman, which is not trivial:

1. **An oral, non-intravenous therapy with a plausible signal.** In a field defined by infusions, ARIA monitoring and amyloid imaging, the paper reports slowed decline on three scales plus plasma Aβ42/40 (p=0.048) and brain-volume effects (p=0.002), with **no neuroimaging adverse events** . A systematic review (CNS Drugs 2026) catalogues the evidence as thin — two RCTs, two papers, two preprints, six conference abstracts — but not as harmful .
2. **The rare-disease statistics defense is peer-reviewed.** The BJCP 2025 paper argues, for exactly these data, that exposure–response modelling and Bayes factors can establish strength of evidence where classical trials cannot . If one accepts that frame, p=0.063 becomes a different sentence.
3. **The company is now, visibly, converting.** RS-005 is a classical n≈170 double-blind; the FDA meeting is about widening it; the CEO who was fired is gone; the new CEO's first word was "alignment."

What the steelman cannot absorb: an **open-label single-group OLE** (registered primary = adverse events) is presented with "LS mean difference" figures at 192 weeks ; the registry was never used; and the threshold-OR headline coexisted for three years with a peer-reviewed p=0.357 on the same co-primary endpoint.

> 【要約】被告側の最善論: ①静脈注射不要の経口薬で、三尺度＋バイオマーカー＋脳容積に信号・ARIA なし ②希少疾病の小データ擁護論（IRT＋ベイズ）は査読済み ③RS-005 で古典設計へ転換中。それでも消せない三事: **単一群 OLE に「LS 平均差」**を載せたこと、**台帳を一度も使わなかった**こと、**OR 見出しが論文の p=0.357 と 3 年間併存**したこと。

## 時代の環境

### 1. レカネマブ＝ドナネマブ時代 — 「効果の閾値」が引き上げられた窓

早期 AD の制薬環境は 2023–2025 に抗アミロイド mAb で再定義された。承認版収録済み:
- lecanemab: 米国 2023-01（加速→通常）／**EU では CHMP 否定**・日本では 2023-09 承認。
- donanemab: 米国 2024-07／**EU CHMP 否定（2025-07）**。

blarcamesine の EU レビューはまさに「**mAb の CDR-SB 差 0.4 前後を CHMP が否定的と評価し始めた 2025 年**」に着地した（2025-11-14）。AD-004 の CDR-SB 差は **−0.483** — mAb 否定事例と同規模の効果量に依存し、しかも機序（sigma-1）はバイオマーカー体系（ARIA もアミロイド低下も）を持たない。**EU で否定された AD 資産は mAb に限らない**ことを本件は最初の非抗体例として記録する（関連: lecanemab・donanemab・パイプライン crenezumab/solanezumab/bace）。

### 2. 「当局に一度も立たない死」の系譜 — 本件はその反例

パイプライン編の AD 資産（solanezumab・crenezumab・bace）は共通して**申請 0・CRL 0・委員会 0**（openFDA NOT_FOUND）で死んだ。blarcamesine も**米国申請は一度も存在しない**（NOT_FOUND・2026-09-11）。しかし本件は**EU に 1 度だけ立って、口頭説明まで受けて否定された**——「裁かれない死」の系譜に対する**「一度だけ裁かれた死」の対**（関連: crenezumab テーマ「当局に立たない死」）。

### 3. EU 取下げ型（再審査の途中降り）— pridopidine との同型

pridopidine: CHMP 否定見解（2025-07-24）→再審査請求→**2025-11-07 会社取下げ**。blarcamesine: CHMP 否定傾向投票（2025-11-14）→再審査方針表明→**2026-03-25 取下げ**。**4 か月違いで同一の型**——「再審査で翻した例は（本収録時点）存在しない」という母数の分母が 2 に増える（関連: pridopidine テーマ「裁かれずに降りる」）。

### 4. 公表しない第 III 相の台帳 — FDAAA 801 の境界

mdma・comp360・seltorexant は「公表遅延／non-Posting」を個別事件として扱った。blarcamesine は**13 試験すべて・12 年・Posting 0** という**母集団そのもの**——個別事件ではなく体質（関連: comp360「審査中 non-Posting」／brepocitinib「勝っても台帳は消えない」＝Posting あり側の対）。

### 5. 閾値三部作の第 IV 章 — 「単位系を変える」統計

pridopidine の「名目 p の 15 年」（閾値・再定義三部作）。AD-004 では**同じデータから**、登録単位系（MMRM 平均差・ADCS-ADL P=0.357）と公表単位系（閾値到達オッズ比 OR 1.84・P=0.015）が同時に生成された。**単位系の選択が結果の正負を決う**構図の極北（関連: pridopidine／comp360「勝った日の裁定」）。

### 6. 小データ擁護論 — レア disease 統計学の実験台

Br J Clin Pharmacol 2025「The new big is small」（PMID 37429704）は blarcamesine の Rett 開発を**IRT＋ベイズ因子で擁護する**査読論文——n=31/n=33/n=92 の三連続試験は、意図せず「小データパラダイム」の学術論争の標本になった。FDA の 2 adequate trials 規範と EMA の希少疾病柔軟性の間に本件は居場所を探し、**RS-005 で n≈170 の古典的対照試験へ回帰**している（2026-09-09 FDA 面談）——「小データの擁護」が「古典回帰」に折れた瞬間の記録。

### 7. バイオテクトックの株主陪審 — ガバナンス 2025-26

emraclidineでは敗北が**買収先の減損**として決算に載った。blarcamesine では**決算ではなく会社そのものが裁判所になった**: CEO の Cause 解任（2026-04-30）→株主訴訟（Rett 開示・却下→控訴）→**取締役会全面交代を求める委任状争奪（2026-08）**。収録時の予言「公開喧嘩」は実現した——ただし**相手は FDA ではなく株主**だった（関連: emraclidine・comp360 の市場規律主題）。

### 8. 日本

jRCT/PMDA 到達不能・10-K に日本の記載なし。日本の席は**空席のまま記録する**（brepocitinib の「日本の席・第四型: 到達不能」に連なる第五型＝**「記載そのものの不在」**候補）。

### 3. AD-004（NCT03790709）— 登録・公表・査読の三冊

#### 3.1 登録
- primary: ①ADAS-Cog（48 週・認知 decline 削減）②ADCS-ADL（48 週・日常能力）。secondary: CDR-SB、AE。
- 52 施設 5 か国。

#### 3.2 公表（: 10-K FY2023／CTAD 2022-12）
- 「co-primary endpoints met」と記載。ただし公表された統計量は**閾値到達オッズ比**: ADAS-Cog 閾値 −0.5 で **OR 1.84・P=0.015**、ADCS-ADL 閾値 +3.5 で **OR 2.67・P=0.0255**。平均差では ADAS −1.85（45% 減速）・P=0.033、CDR-SB −0.42（27%）・P=0.040。「**all statistical analyses performed by external consulting firm**」。
- 2022-07 トップライン当日 PR 原文＝未確認。

#### 3.3 査読（: JPAD 2025・PMID 39800452・2025-01 刊）
- MMRM 主解析・ITT 462（randomized 508・完遂 338=73.2%）:
 - **ADAS-Cog13 LSM 差 −2.027（95%CI −3.522〜−0.533）・P=0.008 → 達成**
 - **ADCS-ADL +0.775（−0.874〜2.423）・P=0.357 → 未達**
 - **CDR-SB（登録上二次）−0.483（−0.853〜−0.114）・P=0.010**
- 論文の自己記述: 「co-primary outcome was met **under the multiplicity control rule** … ADAS-Cog13 P<0.025 **and for CDR-SB** P<0.025, **while ADCS-ADL did not reach significance**」＝片割れ共主項目が落ち、**閉手順で二次項目が救援**。
- バイオマーカー: Aβ42/40 比 上昇 P=0.048・全脳容積減少 減速 P=0.002。安全: serious TEAE **16.7% vs 10.1%**、ARIA 記載なし。
- **同一試験・同一分子で「met」の意味が三冊で食い違う**（CTG=結果なし／10-K=達成（OR）／論文=共主項目 1 本失败＋二次救援）。

#### 3.4 ATTENTION-AD（NCT04314934）
- 単一群 open-label・**登録主項目＝AE**。192 週: ADAS-Cog13 **−3.83・P=0.0165**、ADCS-ADL **+4.30・P=0.0206**（10-K FY2025）。並行対照なし・比較母集団定義＝**未確認**（本文未全文）。
